Use it as a full screen app

Install it on your phone to open it full screen, with no browser bars.

On iPhone, full screen comes from adding it to your Home Screen:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open it from the new icon.
